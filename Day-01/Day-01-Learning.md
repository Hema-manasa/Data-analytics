- Basic Excel structure

- Excel Tables
- Sorting and Filtering

## Dataset

Created a sample sales dataset containing:

- Date
- Product
- Category
- Quantity
- Price
- Revenue
- City

## Analysis

- Total Revenue: ₹19,500
- Total Quantity Sold: 21
- Highest Revenue Category: Footwear
- Highest Revenue Product: Shoes
- Highest Order Count City: Chennai

## Key Learning

Learned the difference between raw data, information, and insights.

Also learned that revenue is different from profit and that business decisions should be based on proper analysis of the available data.
